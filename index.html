ABOUT US

Nearly two decades of choosing well.

Emiliana Parati opened in Baku in 2007 with a simple premise: the finishing materials of a home deserve the same care as its architecture.

We don't chase every trend. We choose ranges we would specify in our own homes — from breathable lime paints to European wallpapers, solid doors and parquet built to outlast a single renovation.

"Good materials don't shout. They just age well."
